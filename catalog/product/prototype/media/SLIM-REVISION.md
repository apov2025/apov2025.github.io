# Slim concept imagery

The 4 × 6 collage and model renders are deterministic Three.js renders of the revised 6.2 mm GLBs, generated by `scripts/render-prototype-collage.mjs`. Colors and camera views are in `colorway-collage.json`. The USB-C geometry retains its original dimensions; the headphone jack is removed.

The photographic concepts were revised with built-in image_gen using the previous generated photograph as edit target and the updated 3D render as geometry reference. They remain labeled as generated concept images on the page.

## concept-front-slim.png

Edit target: first image, a generated concept product photograph. Second image is the updated exact 3D geometry reference. Revise ONLY the device thickness and side profile to match the new 6.2 mm design (was 8.3 mm), about 25% thinner. Keep the full front layout, 76.01 x 125 mm proportions, graphite materials, two right-hand keys, offset blank gray screen, speaker holes, wordmark, pose, framing, background and light unchanged. The top edge has only a power key and NO headphone jack. Preserve a realistic thin continuous glass front and subtly sculpted rear shoulders; no impossible razor edges. Photorealistic concept photograph, no extra objects or text.
